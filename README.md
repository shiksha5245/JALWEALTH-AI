Transforming Environmental Challenges into Data-Driven Economic Opportunities

---

📌 Project Overview

JALWEALTH AI is an integrated Data Science and Artificial Intelligence project designed to explore the business potential of water hyacinth-based products through data-driven analysis.

The project brings together business intelligence, financial analytics, machine learning, revenue forecasting, explainable AI, environmental analytics, and manufacturing performance analysis within a unified analytical framework.

Its primary objective is to transform raw business and operational data into meaningful insights that can help understand product performance, evaluate financial trends, estimate future revenue, and monitor environmental resource utilization.

Developed using Python and Jupyter Notebook, JALWEALTH AI demonstrates how data analytics and AI techniques can support informed decision-making in a sustainability-focused business model.


---
🎯 Problem Statement

Sustainable product-based businesses involve multiple interconnected areas, including product sales, profitability, financial performance, resource utilization, and manufacturing operations.

Analysing these areas independently can make it difficult to understand the overall business performance.

JALWEALTH AI addresses this analytical challenge by integrating multiple data-driven approaches to answer important questions:

- Which products contribute most to revenue and profit?
- How does financial performance change over time?
- What patterns can be identified from historical sales data?
- How can machine learning support revenue forecasting?
- Which features influence the model's predictions?
- How can biomass utilization and manufacturing indicators be analysed?
- How can interactive dashboards make complex business information easier to understand?

---

- Analyse historical sales and financial performance.

2. Product Revenue & Profit Analysis

3. Historical Financial Analytics

- Monthly revenue analysis.
- Monthly profit trends.

4. Predictive Revenue Analytics

- 2026 revenue forecasting.
- Monthly and annual forecast analysis.
- Conservative, baseline, and optimistic scenarios.

5. Machine Learning & Model Evaluation

Multiple models and baseline approaches are evaluated, including:

- Linear Regression
- Ridge Regression
- Gradient Boosting
- Random Forest
- Extra Trees
- Historical Mean Baseline
- Previous Month Naive Baseline

Evaluation metrics:

- Mean Absolute Error (MAE)
- Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE)
- Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE)
- R² Score

Time-series cross-validation and chronological holdout evaluation are used to assess forecasting performance.

6. Explainable AI – SHAP

- Global feature importance analysis.
- Individual prediction explanations.
- Feature contribution analysis.
- Improved interpretability of machine learning predictions.

7. Environmental Analytics

- Biomass processing analysis.
- Reported waste-diversion indicators.
- Resource utilization metrics.

8. Manufacturing & Artisan Analytics

- Finished product analysis.
- Monthly production indicators.
- Processing utilization.
- Artisan employment days.

⚙️ Analytical Workflow

The project follows a structured analytical workflow:

1. Data Input: Load business, sales, financial, environmental, and product-related data.
2. Data Preparation: Inspect, clean, transform, and prepare data.
3. Exploratory Data Analysis: Identify patterns, distributions, and relationships.
4. Business Intelligence: Analyse products, revenue, profit, and financial performance.
5. Predictive Modelling: Train and evaluate forecasting models.
6. Explainable AI: Interpret model predictions using SHAP.
7. Environmental & Manufacturing Analysis: Evaluate resource and production indicators.
8. Visualization: Present analytical results through dashboards and charts.

🔍 Model Explainability

JALWEALTH AI incorporates SHAP to understand the contribution of different features to machine learning predictions.

Important features identified in the global feature-importance analysis include:

- Month Number
- Revenue Lag 6
- Month Cos
- Revenue Lag 1
- Month Sin
- Revenue Lag 2

This provides additional transparency into model behaviour rather than treating predictions as unexplained outputs.

- Scenario adjustments of ±5% are sensitivity assumptions, not statistical prediction intervals.
- Model performance can vary across evaluation metrics and time periods.
- Environmental indicators require consistent definitions and measurement validation.
- The current implementation is an analytical MVP, not a fully deployed commercial platform.
